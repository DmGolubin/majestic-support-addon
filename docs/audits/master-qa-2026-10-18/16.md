- New scope tests model scope removal within the same account/generation; session revocation and identity removal are covered separately in existing controller/lifecycle tests. Scope-return fixtures are synthetic and do not assert real server permission restoration behavior.
- Q09/index startup and UI-state recovery remain parent-owned and were not modified or re-diagnosed. Shared working-tree changes can continue after this snapshot.
